# Social brand marks

Instagram, TikTok, iFood and WhatsApp SVG marks come from
[Simple Icons 16.36.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.36.0/icons),
distributed under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.36.0/LICENSE.md).
The paths are unchanged; their published brand color is applied to the SVG root.

The 99 mark comes from [Wikimedia Commons: Logotipo da 99.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Logotipo_da_99.svg).
Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.
All assets are served locally; visitor requests do not contact the icon providers.
